# Licences and attribution

This page records the licences of everything judgly ships and uses, with the
evidence for each. judgly is a small hobby project that puts a
calibration head on top of an open model run through llama.cpp. The idea of
typed, calibrated "System One" decisions comes from TypeSafe's Jev; the heavy
lifting is done by llama.cpp/ggml, the Gemma and Qwen models, yyjson, and the
datasets and papers listed below. None of it is judgly's invention.

Evidence checked: 2026-09-26 and 2026-09-27 (on 2026-09-27: FEVER, SNLI, WANLI, SciNLI,
HelpSteer2, LEDGAR, the fresh final tier, the bench tier and the candidates in
[section 2.1](#21-candidates-not-used-and-why)). Scope: every file in the wheel and the sdist,
every dataset used for fitting, the models and the GGUF repositories the packs
download from, and NOTICE and LICENSES/. Evidence was fetched from the primary
source where one could be reached; where it could not, this is said. **Items
marked FLAG are residual uncertainties in the licence evidence.**

I am not a lawyer; this records what the sources say, not legal advice.

**Model-written data** (one rule, also in the registry header): text written by a model, with or
without human revision, may be fitted on when its labels are human judgments and the dataset's
licence is allowed; labels written by a model (a model's rating, preference, verdict or soft
gold distribution) are never fitted on, whatever the licence. WANLI (GPT-3 text, crowdworker
labels) is fitted under it; UltraFeedback and Prometheus (GPT-4 labels) are excluded under it
(section 2.1); typed-decisions (a teacher model's gold) and JevBench (items and answers written
by models) are evaluation only. The rule does not settle a model provider's terms of use, which
bind whoever called the model (for WANLI, the dataset's authors); judgly does not call those
models, and a calibration head is not a competing language model.

No fitting source is non-commercial, "unknown" or without a licence. Sources
the policy does not allow for fitting (ARC CC-BY-SA, OpenBookQA unknown, SciQ
CC-BY-NC, RACE other, dair-ai/emotion other, `pietrolesci/nli_fever` no licence on
its card) are recorded in the registry and are not fitted on.
